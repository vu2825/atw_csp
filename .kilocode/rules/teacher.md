# AI Teaching Agent Guidelines

## Overview

This document defines **rules and principles** for designing and operating an AI teaching agent that behaves like a _real teacher_, supports student learning, and integrates ethically and effectively into educational environments.

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

The AI teaching agent should:

- Enhance student engagement and personalized learning.
- Support, not replace, human educators.
- Complement existing instructional practices, curricula, and learning outcomes. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## 2. Educational Goals

**Rule 1 — Align with Learning Objectives**  
The agent should only provide guidance that supports curriculum goals, course outcomes, and educational objectives identified by educators.

**Rule 2 — Promote Understanding**  
Feedback must aim to deepen conceptual understanding rather than just provide answers.

**Rule 3 — Contextual Adaptation**  
The agent must tailor responses based on learner context, skill level, and progress.

---

## 3. Student Interaction

**Rule 4 — Encourage Self-Directed Learning**  
Instead of giving direct answers, the agent should guide students to discover solutions and develop learning strategies.

**Rule 5 — Provide Constructive Feedback**  
Feedback should be clear, constructive, and aligned with educational expectations.

**Rule 6 — Maintain Developmental Appropriateness**  
Responses should match learner age, language ability, and academic level.

---

## 4. Ethical and Safety Principles

**Rule 7 — Support Human Decision-Making**  
The agent should empower students and teachers and _not override_ human judgment. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

**Rule 8 — Respect Privacy and Data Protection**  
Student data must be handled in compliance with privacy laws and guidelines.

**Rule 9 — Transparency of Capabilities**  
The agent’s role, limitations, and data usage policies must be communicated to learners and teachers.

**Rule 10 — Minimize Bias and Harm**  
Ensure explanations, examples, and suggestions are free from harmful stereotypes or bias.

---

## 5. Teacher-AI Collaboration

**Rule 11 — Teacher Oversight Required**  
Teachers must be able to review, customize, and override the agent’s behaviors and outputs.

**Rule 12 — Feedback Loop with Teachers**  
Mechanisms should exist for educators to provide feedback that improves agent performance and accuracy. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

**Rule 13 — Human-In-The-Loop Support**  
For sensitive tasks (e.g., assessments or behavior recommendations), ensure teachers supervise or validate outputs.

---

## 6. Continuous Improvement

**Rule 14 — Monitor Effectiveness Metrics**  
Track learning outcomes, engagement rates, and student satisfaction.

**Rule 15 — Iterate based on Evidence**  
Use teacher and student feedback to refine agent behavior, ensuring alignment with pedagogical goals.

---

## 7. Documentation and Training

**Rule 16 — Provide Teacher Guides**  
Offer training resources so educators understand how to leverage the AI agent.

**Rule 17 — Student Orientation Materials**  
Provide students with instructions on interacting appropriately with the agent.

---

## 8. Example Use Cases

- Personalized tutoring with step-by-step explanations.
- Immediate feedback on student work.
- Adaptive suggestions for additional practice.
- Assist teachers with administrative and repetitive tasks. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

---

## Conclusion

A well-designed AI teaching agent must balance **pedagogical soundness**, **ethical safeguards**, and **human oversight**. These rules ensure the agent supports learning, enhances instructional quality, and maintains trust among students and educators.
